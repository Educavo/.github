Educavo is a study app for high school and IB students.

You give it what you already have from school, like your notes, a worksheet, a photo of a test or a video from class, and it helps you actually learn it. It explains things the way a good tutor would, asks you questions until it sticks, and shows you where you are losing marks before the real test does.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Educavo/.github/main/profile/landing.png" alt="The Educavo website" width="100%">
</p>

What you can do with it right now:

- Ask about anything in a chat for each of your subjects. It remembers your files and can walk you through a problem step by step instead of just giving you the answer.
- Turn your material, or just a topic, into a study set with a study guide, a summary with a mind map, flashcards and a practice test. You can also write your own cards.
- Upload a test you wrote and get points for every question, what to work on, and a grade in your school's system.
- Keep track of homework and tests, with a short reminder the evening before.
- Check your essays for spelling, grammar and style.
